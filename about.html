Myofunctional Therapy

Orofacial myofunctional therapy addresses how the muscles of the mouth and face work together to support breathing, swallowing, chewing, and jaw comfort. Through personalized exercises and guided education, it can help with concerns such as mouth breathing, tongue thrust, clenching, orthodontic relapse, and sleep quality, for both children and adults.
