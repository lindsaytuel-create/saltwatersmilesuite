Myofunctional Therapy

Simple daily exercises that retrain how the tongue, lips, and face rest and move, for easier breathing, better sleep, healthy swallowing, and orthodontic results that last.

How It Works Refer a Patient

Small habits, lasting change

Your tongue, lips, and face repeat the same patterns thousands of times a day. When those patterns are off, they can affect breathing, swallowing, sleep, and where your teeth settle.

Therapy retrains them. No devices and no surgery, just short daily exercises and regular visits until the new habits become automatic.

What it helps with

Breathing & Sleep

  • Mouth breathing
  • Snoring and restless sleep
  • Waking up tired

Oral Habits

  • Tongue thrust and swallowing
  • Thumb, finger, or pacifier habits
  • Low tongue posture

Orthodontics & Growth

  • Open bite and crowding
  • Teeth shifting after treatment
  • A narrow or high palate

Jaw Comfort

  • Clenching and grinding
  • TMJ pain
  • Jaw, face, and neck tension

Tongue-Tie & Surgery

  • Before and after a tongue-tie release
  • Support around jaw surgery

For orthodontic patients

Myo & orthodontics

Your tongue presses on your teeth every time you swallow, hundreds of times a day. When it rests low or pushes forward, it works against the teeth your orthodontist is moving, and can let them drift once treatment ends.

Therapy retrains those habits so they support orthodontic treatment, and we keep your orthodontist updated along the way.

Orthodontists: refer a patient →

Before treatment

Build the foundation

  • Nose breathing and a closed-lip rest
  • The tongue resting on the roof of the mouth
  • Ending thumb or pacifier habits

During treatment

Work alongside it

  • Retrain a tongue-thrust swallow
  • Support open bite treatment
  • Exercises work with appliances in place

After treatment

Help results hold

  • Make the new habits automatic
  • Help keep teeth from drifting back
  • Works alongside your retainer

Your orthodontist stays in charge. Therapy can start at any stage and works side by side with orthodontic treatment, supporting the results your orthodontist is working toward.
