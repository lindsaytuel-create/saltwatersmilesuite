Privacy Policy

What this website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

This policy covers information collected through saltwatersmilesuite.com. Health information you share with us as a patient is protected separately, as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information you choose to send us

Contact form

When you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and your message. The form is delivered to us by a service called Formspree. Please don’t include medical details in the contact form. We’re happy to discuss those privately by phone or at your visit.

Online booking and gift cards

Appointments are booked through Acuity Scheduling, and gift cards are purchased through Square. When you use those pages, the information you enter is collected by those companies under their own privacy policies, and shared with us so we can schedule and serve you.

Referral and consultation forms

Referrals sent from our Refer a Patient page, including any photos or files attached, and requests sent from our Request a Consultation page go directly to our Google Workspace account, which is covered by a HIPAA business associate agreement with Google, and are handled as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices. Providers can also print the form instead of sending it.

Patient portal

Myofunctional therapy patients sign in to a patient portal provided by MyoMessenger to see their weekly plan, exercise videos, and appointments. The portal is a separate website, reached through the Sign In button on our Patient Portal page, and information there is handled as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information collected automatically

We don’t use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of our own on this website.

Some features load content from other companies, which may receive your IP address and basic browser information when the content loads:

  • Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs for security.
  • Our fonts are provided by Google Fonts.
  • Videos on our Research page are embedded from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Links to Google Maps and to our Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook pages take you to those sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

How we use your information

We use the information you send us to answer your questions, schedule and provide services, send appointment-related messages, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with others for their marketing.

Children

We care for children as well as adults, but this website is not meant for children under 13 to send us information. A parent or guardian should contact us on a child’s behalf.

Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, which is one reason we ask you not to include health details in website forms.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete contact information you have sent through this website by reaching out using the details below. Health records are handled as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
