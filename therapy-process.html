How It Works

What to expect

A clear, simple path from your first conversation to habits that last.

Request a Consultation Refer a Patient

Four steps

01

Consultation

Short conversation · No commitment

We talk through what you or your provider have noticed and decide together whether an evaluation makes sense.

02

Evaluation

One visit · In person

A hands-on look at breathing, tongue posture, swallowing, and the muscles of the face and jaw. You and your referring provider receive the findings and a plan.

03

Therapy

Typically 10–14 visits · Mostly over Zoom

Each visit adds a few new exercises and checks your progress. Practice at home takes a few minutes, twice a day.

04

Completion

Re-assessment · Check-ins

When the new habits hold on their own, therapy ends. We check in afterward and send a closing summary to your provider.

For children: a parent or caregiver joins visits and helps with practice at home. Folding it into a routine, like brushing teeth, makes it easy.

Common questions

How long does therapy take?

Most programs are 10 to 14 visits, spaced one to a few weeks apart. Your evaluation sets the actual plan.

How old does my child need to be?

Usually four or older, old enough to follow simple exercises with a parent’s help at home.

Can I do therapy during orthodontic treatment?

Yes. The exercises work with appliances in place, and we time therapy to your stage of treatment.

Do I still need my retainer?

Yes. Therapy works alongside your retainer, not instead of it.

Will you work with my orthodontist or dentist?

Yes. We send our findings and progress updates to the provider who referred you.

Is it covered by insurance?

Dental insurance usually doesn’t cover it, though some medical plans may. We’ll go over costs and payment options at your consultation.

Ready to start?

We’re taking consultation requests ahead of our 2027 opening. Providers can send a referral online.
