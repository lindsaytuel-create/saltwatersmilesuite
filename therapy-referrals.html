For Orthodontists & Referring Providers

Refer a patient

A quick referral is all it takes. We contact the family, then keep your office updated with written findings. Therapy begins in 2027.

Go to Referral Form

How to refer

1

Send the referral

Fill in the form below and press Send. It comes straight to us. You can print a copy for the family too.

Fill in the form

Complete the referral form below, then print it or save it as a PDF. A note on your own letterhead works too.

2

We reach out to the patient

We contact the patient or family directly to schedule a consultation, and let your office know once we’ve connected.

Send it with the family

Give the referral to the family to bring to their consultation, or call us to arrange secure delivery. We then reach out to schedule.

3

You receive findings

Evaluation results come back to you before therapy begins, so you can weigh in on the plan.

What your office receives: an evaluation summary before therapy begins, progress updates timed to your treatment, and a completion report. After the in-person evaluation, most visits are over Zoom, so families outside Tillamook can take part. We provide myofunctional therapy only, and refer back to you when findings fall outside our scope.
